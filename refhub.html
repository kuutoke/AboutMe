Artist Reference Hub

A decade ago I started collecting screenshots, links, bookmarks, infographics- anything and everything I stumbled across that made me learn something about art and how it works. All that time of unplanned collection, though, led to a lot of misplaced data, disorganized docs full of links, and worst case: now-defunct webpages. Spurred by a school database project, I decided to actually make my idealized Artist Reference Hub. Part organizational and searching tool, part media preservation, part technique discovery.

Planned features are filtering and searching. For now, though, I'm focusing on barebones functionality and finding the happy median between just enough data and preserving entire websites.
